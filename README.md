## 📌 Executive Summary

MoveInSync powers the daily commutes of **1.5M+ enterprise employees across 430+ organizations**. This repository contains the complete empirical diagnosis, interactive intelligence dashboard, and prioritized engineering action plan to recover and elevate MoveInSync's Google Play Store ratings from the recent **3.77 run-rate back to 4.60+ / 5.0**.

All findings are grounded in **306 verified public Play Store reviews** extracted directly via Google's batch RPC protocol (`oCPfdb`) for package `com.moveinsync.ets`.

## 🎯 Key Deliverables

### 1. [Strategy & Engineering Roadmap](MoveInSync_FDE_PlayStore_Rating_Strategy.md)
The complete proposal for Product & Engineering leadership:
* **The "Misdirected Anger" Paradox:** 50.5% of negative reviews are fleet/vendor operational delays vented on the Play Store due to unassisted in-app escalation.
* **UX Dark Patterns:** Identifying the unskippable post-trip ride rating modal lock and the intrusive "Seatbelt Safety" popup intercepting OTP clicks during boarding.
* **3-Horizon Roadmap:** Jira-ready specifications for **P0 Quick Wins (Week 1)**, **P1 Core Fixes (Weeks 2–4)**, and **P2 Operational Deflection (Weeks 4–6)**.
